# Executive Decision Memo: Retail Sales & Profitability Analytics

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Gross Revenue:** $5,756.37
- **Net Profit:** $1,857.37
- **Blended Profit Margin:** 32.27%
- **Order Return Rate:** 25.00%

## 2. Key Insights
1. **Product Concentration:** The *Laptop Pro 15* drives the bulk of gross revenue ($3,349.97) but yields the lowest net margin (~22.39%).
2. **High-Margin Drivers:** Peripherals and ergonomic office lines capture superior profitability (upwards of 62.49% margin).
3. **Logistical Vulnerability:** Late deliveries account for the primary share of return revenue loss, indicating supply chain bottlenecks.

## 3. Recommended Actions
- Bundle low-margin flagship hardware with high-margin accessories.
- Implement strict SLA thresholds with regional delivery carriers to reduce transit delays.
- Establish batch-level QA checks on suppliers with elevated defect rates.
